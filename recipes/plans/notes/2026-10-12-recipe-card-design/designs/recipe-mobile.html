Butternut Squash Soup

Creamy and warming

Ingredients

  • 1Butternut squash
  • 1Onion
  • 500 mlVegetable stock
  • 2 tbspCream

Method

  1. 01Heat the oven to 200°C. Halve the squash, scoop out the seeds, and roast cut-side down for about 40 minutes, until soft.
  2. 02Meanwhile, sauté the chopped onion in a little oil over medium heat until soft and golden, about 10 minutes.
  3. 03Scoop the squash into the pot, add the stock, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. 04Blend until smooth. Stir in the cream and season to taste.

Notes

Roasting the squash first gives it a deeper, nuttier flavor than boiling. Freezes well — make a double batch.
